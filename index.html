Pimple Popper

Official Support & Privacy Policy page for the Pimple Popper mobile game (Bundle ID: com.arikweb.pimplepopper) by arik-web.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 14, 2026

Pimple Popper (published by arik-web) does not require user registration or an account, and does not directly collect, store, or share personal information. Your game progress, scores, and settings are stored locally on your device only.

Third-Party Services

The app integrates Unity Ads and Unity In-App Purchasing:

  • Unity Ads: May collect device identifiers (such as IDFA on iOS with App Tracking Transparency permission, or Android Advertising ID), coarse location/IP address, and app usage/diagnostic data to serve and measure advertisements, prevent fraud, and analyze performance. Review Unity's Privacy Policy at https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.
  • In-App Purchases: All payment transactions (such as the optional "Remove Ads Forever" non-consumable purchase) are processed directly by the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. We do not receive or store your billing or payment details—only a confirmation of whether the entitlement is active.

Children's Privacy

The app is intended for a general / teen audience (12+ / Teen) and does not knowingly collect personal data from children or serve behaviorally targeted ads to children.

Data Deletion & Retention

Local game data can be deleted at any time by using the in-app Reset button or by uninstalling the application. For inquiries regarding data processed by Unity Ads, please see Unity's privacy documentation or contact us below.
